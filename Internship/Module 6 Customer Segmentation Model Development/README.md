## Activities & Processes
1. **Exploratory Cluster Analysis**: Performed feature selection and plotted distributions (pair plots, correlation heatmaps, PCA, and box plots) to determine segment characteristics.
2. **Optimal Cluster Count Determination**: Ran WCSS elbow curve analysis and calculated silhouette scores for $K \in [2, 10]$. K-Means at $K=2$ yielded the highest silhouette score (~0.25) and best cluster separation.
3. **Algorithm Comparative Evaluation**: Compared K-Means, Hierarchical Clustering, DBSCAN, and GMM models. DBSCAN resulted in noise detection (0 clusters). K-Means and Hierarchical shared a Silhouette Score of 0.238, while GMM had 0.244. K-Means was selected for its high interpretability and stability.
4. **Stability Auditing**: Tested silhouette metrics across bootstrap samples, various random seeds, and random states, confirming that K-Means predictions are highly stable (scores remaining constant at ~0.25).
5. **Cluster Profiling**: Profiled the two resulting clusters:
   - **Cluster 0** (High-Value Elite): Higher Income, higher Total Spending, lower Family Size, and high store/catalog purchases.
   - **Cluster 1** (Value-Conscious Frugal): Lower Income, lower overall spending, larger families, and reliance on web purchases and discount deals.
