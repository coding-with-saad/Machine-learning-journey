## Activities & Processes
1. **Segment Sizing and Distribution Analysis**: Computed customer counts and percentages for the clustered dataset to understand the client distribution.
2. **Customer Persona Definition**: Created data-backed descriptions for each segment:
   - **High-Value Elite (Cluster 0)**: High-income professionals, low sensitivity to discounts, high average basket value, frequenting catalog and store purchases.
   - **Value/Frugal Buyers (Cluster 1)**: Middle-to-low income households, high responsiveness to campaigns and discount promotions, purchasing mainly via web.
3. **Business Opportunity Analysis**: Formulated estimates of segment potential, average spending ratios, and potential revenue lifts from targeted promotions.
4. **Marketing Strategy Design**: Structured campaigns, channels, and promotions tailored to the personas (e.g. VIP loyalty incentives and direct mail catalogs for Elite; email discount codes, discount campaigns, and value bundles for Frugal Buyers).
